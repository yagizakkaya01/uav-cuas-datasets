# Pilot Region Comparison: Eindhoven vs Gatwick vs Aalborg vs Akrotiri

*Prepared by Yağız Akkaya, 7 November 2026. Based on the proposals in this folder. The team has not decided yet.*

| Criterion | Eindhoven (Yağız Akkaya) | Gatwick (Yaren Duyan) | Aalborg (Aydan Doğan) | Akrotiri / Chania, Crete (Mert Gökalp) | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Facility type / NATO theme** | Strong: joint civil–military airfield (Eindhoven Air Base, NATO MRTT fleet) | Weak: civil airport only | Strong: joint civil–military airfield (Flyvestation Aalborg) | Strong: Chania Airport (joint civil–military, 115 Combat Wing), NSA Souda Bay and NAMFI (NATO missile firing range) in one AOI | Eindhoven = Aalborg = Akrotiri |
| **Documented drone incident** | Strong: 22 Nov 2025 closure, C-UAS activated; Volkel the night before; parliamentary record; IISS study (Jul 2026) | Strong: Dec 2018, ~30–33 h closure; official Sussex PCC review; academic papers | Good: 24–25 Sep 2025 (~3 h closure, lit drones, Reuters); 26 Sep closure still to verify; IISS covers the Danish wave | Weak: no hostile drone incursion found (Greece not in the IISS list); closest events are the Mar 2026 Souda emergency alert, US sea-drone patrols and counter-drone live firing at NAMFI | Eindhoven |

| **Main weakness** | PDOK zone polygons not yet retrieved; real military site → vulnerability outputs must stay out of the public repo | Civil only; building heights must be derived | Data access untested; no restriction-zone API; real military site (same publication caveat) | No drone incident; coarse 30 m terrain; real military sites (same publication caveat) | — |

**Summary:** Eindhoven leads on building data (3DBAG) and the incident record; Akrotiri has the most hands-on data work and hilly terrain that suits line-of-sight analysis, but no drone incident and only 30 m terrain; Gatwick has the best-tested restriction-zone data and peer-reviewed literature but is civil only; Aalborg matches Eindhoven's civil–military profile and links best to visual detection, but nothing has been tested yet. Suggested next step: run the 5-step acceptance check on Aalborg; if it passes, the team chooses between Eindhoven and Aalborg, with Gatwick used for motivation. The team has not decided.

The pilot region is used only by the decision engine; demo measurements are taken on campus and never presented as if collected at the pilot region.
