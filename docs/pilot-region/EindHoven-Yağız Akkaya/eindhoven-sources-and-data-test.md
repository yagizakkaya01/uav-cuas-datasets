# Eindhoven: Incident Sources and Data Test

*Prepared by Yağız Akkaya, 6 November 2026. All sources were accessed on this date; entries marked "read" were checked for content.*

## 1. Incident timeline

| Date | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 21 Nov 2025, 19:00–21:00 | At Volkel Air Base guards first saw 5, then 10 drones. It was dark; the military fired from the ground; the drones flew off and were not found. | Omroep Brabant (news 3), Kamerstuk 36 858 |
| 22 Nov 2025, ~20:30–22:40 | Multiple drones were sighted at Eindhoven Airport. All civil and military flights were halted and diverted to Weeze, Rotterdam and Brussels. Ministry of Defence counter-drone systems were on standby. | Omroep Brabant (news 2), Al Jazeera |
| 26 Nov 2025 | In the Defence Committee the Minister said the drones were "not only seen by eye", an attempt was made to bring them down, and Eindhoven was closed to avoid any risk. | Kamerstuk 36 858 no. 6 |
| 7 Dec 2025 | Another drone at Volkel; two F-35s were scrambled. | NOS, IISS |
| 26 Jan 2026 | The Royal Marechaussee inquiry stalled: no suspect, no drone recovered, no criminal investigation opened. | Omroep Brabant (news 4) |
| Jul 2026 | IISS report: 144 incidents in 13 countries between August 2024 and February 2026. It assesses that the Volkel/Eindhoven drones most likely launched from Russia-linked "shadow fleet" ships off the Dutch coast. | IISS, NOS |

## 4. Academic and think-tank sources

**No peer-reviewed journal article analysing the Eindhoven/Volkel incident was found.** The incident is recent (November 2025); the most rigorous analyses available are think-tank reports:

1. **IISS**, C. Edwards, R. Fox O'Loughlin, L. Bearn, *Russia's UAV Campaign Over Europe*, July 2026. *Read.* Eindhoven and Volkel on pp. 11, 13, 23–24 (Map 2.6). Method: ACLED + AIS ship tracks + open sources + interviews with officials, 144 incidents. This is the strongest source to cite, and it is the "IISS Report (2026)" already listed in Layer 1. Copy accessed: https://www.astrid-online.it/static/upload/russ/russia-uav-campaign-over-europe.pdf (the official IISS link still needs to be added).
2. **ECFR**, U. Franke, *Buzzkill: How to defend Europe against drones*, policy brief, 17 Aug 2026. *Read.* Does not mention Eindhoven, but directly supports our design: radar struggles with small drones, birds cause false alarms, electro-optical sensors need line of sight, jamming near airports affects all systems; it recommends sensor fusion. https://ecfr.eu/publication/buzzkill-how-to-defend-europe-against-drones/
3. **BISI**, M. Asllani, *Probing the Gaps: The Drone Intelligence Threat in Europe*, 18 Nov 2025. *Read.* Published before the incident and does not mention the Netherlands; general context. https://bisi.org.uk/reports/probing-the-gaps-the-drone-intelligence-threat-in-europe

Suggested use: cite IISS for the incident and ECFR for the design rationale (fusion, line of sight). Peer-reviewed C-UAS method papers are already in Layers 3–4.

## 5. Data and API test (6 November 2026)

The project's cloud environment blocks Dutch servers, so these tests were run through a remote fetch tool.

| Source | Result | What was tested |
|---|:---:|---|
| **3DBAG API** (`api.3dbag.nl`) | ✅ | `collections` returns one collection (`pand`), CC BY 4.0. `items` query for the AOI bbox (RD New `152761,383434,157279,386772`): **6,946 buildings**. Every sampled building has LoD 0, 1.2, 1.3 and 2.2 geometry with `b3_h_dak_max` (roof) and `b3_h_maaiveld` (ground). Example: roof 29.3 m, ground 21.5 m (NAP). CRS EPSG:7415. |
| **AHN** (PDOK ATOM) | ✅ catalogue | `service.pdok.nl/rws/ahn/atom/index.xml` lists "DSM 0.5m" and "DTM 0.5m", licence CC0. Tile download not yet tested. |
| **OpenSky REST** | ✅ | `states/all` for the airport box (`51.40–51.50, 5.30–5.50`): 3 aircraft, including callsign `MMF25` (Dutch-registered, on approach at 38 m) and a Wizz Air aircraft on the ground. MMF is assumed to be the NATO Multinational MRTT Fleet callsign (inferred). Historical data (e.g. the night of 22 Nov 2025) requires a Trino account. |
| **PDOK drone no-fly zones** (`api.pdok.nl/lvnl/drone-no-flyzones/ogc/v1`) | ⚠️ | Landing page and schema work. Owner LVNL, licence PDM 1.0. Layers: `luchtvaartgebieden` (fields `localtype`, `source_txt`, polygon) and `landingsite`. `collections` and `items` queries failed through the remote tool, so the polygons were not retrieved. |
| **Overture buildings** (S3) | ✅ | Tested earlier: 4,171 buildings in the AOI, 543 with height values. |
