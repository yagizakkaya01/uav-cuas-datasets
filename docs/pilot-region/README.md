# Pilot Region Proposals

> **Status (7 Nov 2026): not yet decided.** Four candidates are on the table: **Eindhoven Airport (NL)**, **London Gatwick Airport (UK)**, **Aalborg Airport / Air Base (DK)** and **Akrotiri / Chania, Crete (GR)**. Helsinki was proposed earlier and superseded by Gatwick.

The pilot region is used **only by the decision engine** (risk grid, line of sight, routing, map UI). The physical demo runs on campus (see [`../hardware/`](../hardware/)) and its measurements are never presented as if they were taken at the pilot region.

## Points all proposals agree on

- Geodata, open sensor datasets and local demo recordings are **three separate evidence sources**; none is presented as collected at the pilot region.
- Before the final decision, run the **5-step acceptance check** from the Helsinki/Gatwick proposals on a small sample area: (1) elevation, building and OSM files download, (2) licence and source dates recorded, (3) layers open in the same place, (4) gaps and road connectivity checked, (5) file sizes manageable on team laptops. Log results in [`../verified-access-log.md`](../verified-access-log.md).
- The repository is public: **do not publish detailed vulnerability outputs** (blind spots, weakest approach corridors) for a real airfield here. Keep methods and sources in the repo; show site-specific risk maps only in the report and demo.

## Recommendation so far

Yağız Akkaya's review (22 Oct): keep **Eindhoven** as the pilot region because of the military angle, 0.5 m LiDAR and 3DBAG; use the **Gatwick 2018 incident** in the introduction/motivation and list Gatwick as a strong second candidate. Aalborg (added 7 Nov) has the same civil–military profile as Eindhoven and the strongest link to visual detection, but its DHM download still needs a free token and its restriction zones have no usable dataset (7 Nov test). Akrotiri (added 7 Nov) offers hilly terrain that suits line-of-sight analysis and three NATO-relevant sites, but has no documented drone incident and only 30 m terrain data. If the team picks Gatwick, building heights must be derived by Yaren Duyan (risk engine) from the DSM. **The team has not voted yet.**
